About LGU Castilla

Our Mission

To pursue a holistic development of Castilla in an ecologically-sound environment through the convergent efforts in the provision of quality basic services and equitable distribution of resources and opportunities.


Our Vision

A premier agri-ecotourism and industrial destination in Bicol, with God-loving, empowered and healthy citizenry in a progressive economy, with resilient infrastructure and sustainable ecosystem under a just, responsive and participatory local governance.
